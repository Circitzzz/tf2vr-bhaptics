# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-21

- First release.
- Shared `TF2VR.SensoryEvents` 1.0.0 game mod: hits with direction and type, weapon fire and reload,
  jump, double jump, wall jump, landing, wallrun, slide, health, embark, disembark, eject, death and respawn.
- One-click installer: finds Titanfall 2 through Steam libraries, supports both TF2VR and Northstar profiles,
  generates the damage hook from your installed Northstar, adds `-allowlocalhttp`, and installs
  shortcuts, repair and uninstall.
- bHaptics bridge: TactSuit X40 + Tactosy effects, tested in TF2VR. Shows as "Titanfall 2" in bHaptics Player.
